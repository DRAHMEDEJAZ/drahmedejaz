Dr. Ahmed Ejaz is the Founder of Rite Institute of Mind Sciences™ (RIMS) and Similia Homoeo Clinix™, and currently serves as Co-Founder & Advisor at Creative Clusters Marketing LLC and BillingBell Healthcare Systems in the United States. He holds an MBA in Marketing Management, an M.Sc. in Psychology, and a Gold Medal BHMS, and is a Registered Homoeopathic Practitioner (DHMS, National Council for Homoeopathy, Pakistan). He has delivered keynote addresses in 13 countries and trained over 200 physicians and healthcare providers and 500 coaches, counselors, and educators through corporate workshops and one-on-one mentoring. He is co-author of two books, “Rite Healing” and “Mindfulness,” and has published peer-reviewed research on psychology and digital leadership, including a chapter in “Digital Leadership” (THE APRA Publications, 2022). A former Assistant Governor of Rotary International District 3272 (Pakistan) and Paul Harris Fellow, he is now a member of the Rotary Club of Woodbridge, VA.
